Along with the boom of AI usage, and the people who keeps babbling "this is the future" without knowing exactly what they are talking about, one thing is clear--in order to survive, and especially to live, being _efficient_ is not the way for me anymore. I tried it, I lived it, but before I achieved anything, I kept breaking. I tried prolonging it, I tried the hustle mindset, I tried always going against the current, it just does not work for me.

What I enjoy lately was to **take things slowly as I can**. This is now my form of relaxation. I only need to do it as slow as possible in order to truly rest.

I am free from any garbage this ultra capitalist society shoves in my mouth. I am not built for _locking in_, I am built for _dilly-dallying_ my whole life.

I will forever be curious on what I can do, and what are my limits. I want to see more of my self, how much more can I do? And how much more can I achieve?
